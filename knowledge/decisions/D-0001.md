# D-0001 — Learned-recipe crafting convenience

Accepted September 20, 2026 (September 21 UTC).

Rusty requested a lightweight mod for Survival players to look up and craft items
through a Creative-style interface, filtered by recipes already known. They
selected "Consume ingredients—convenient crafting" and "Require a nearby crafting
table" for 3×3 recipes. Craftlight is the proposed name under their delegation.

The server owns unlock, ingredient, workbench and output validation. Reuse the
actual Minecraft recipe and crafting-menu paths so tags, recipe alternatives,
components and crafting remainders retain vanilla behavior. Smaller inventory
recipes remain available without a table. Other processing recipes are browsable
but retain their station, fuel and processing rules. No automatic recursive
crafting or storage-network integration is part of this request.

The client catalog is cached and filtered on interaction, with a bounded visible
grid. The mod adds no world tick scans or mandatory recipe-viewer dependency.
Tests cover locked/known recipes, ingredient costs and remainders, full inventory,
workbench distance/removal, and forged requests, plus a hardware shader-client
walkthrough. This decision approves implementation, not publication/deployment.
